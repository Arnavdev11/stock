Market breadth (sample)

Gainers and losers

  • Top gainers / top losers today
  • Up or down 4%+ with high volume
  • Gap up / gap down at open
  • Weekly and monthly gainers

Volume

  • Volume 2x or more of the 20-day average
  • Highest traded value (₹) today
  • Quiet stocks about to move (low volume dry-up)

Highs and lows

  • 52-week high / 52-week low
  • All-time high
  • Near 52-week high (within 5%)
  • 20-day high breakout

Technical

  • Above 20 / 50 / 200 day average
  • Golden cross, MACD crossover
  • RSI above 60 or below 30
  • Supertrend, inside bar, Bollinger squeeze

Fundamental

  • PE, market cap, dividend yield
  • ROE, ROCE, debt to equity
  • Sales and profit growth
  • Promoter holding, quarterly results

Sectors

  • Sector gainers and losers
  • Sector strength this week and month
  • Stocks above 50 DMA per sector

Roadmap: step by step

Weeks 1–2 · Plan

Validate and set up

  • Niche is set: swing traders who hold for days to weeks
  • Talk to 20 traders, ask what they pay for today
  • Register company, buy domain, talk to a CA/lawyer about SEBI
Weeks 3–12 · Build

MVP (first version)

  • End-of-day data from a licensed vendor (no live feed needed)
  • 4 pages: Swing scans, Market breadth, Sector strength, Watchlist
  • Login, free vs paid plan, payments
  • Fast web app that works well on mobile
Months 4–6 · Launch

Go live

  • Beta with 100 users, fix bugs
  • YouTube, Telegram, X, WhatsApp communities
  • Weekly market-breadth posts to build trust
Months 7–12 · Grow

Add depth

  • Evening Telegram/email digest, custom scans, chart patterns
  • % of stocks above 50/200 DMA, FII/DII, results calendar
  • Broker tie-ups, mobile app
Year 2 · Scale

Become a brand

  • Backtesting, API for developers
  • Hindi and regional language versions
  • Education courses, B2B for brokers

How you earn

Subscriptions

Free plan with delay and limits. Pro at about ₹299–999/month. This is your main income.

Broker referrals

Earn when users open demat accounts. Always disclose it clearly.

Evening alerts and API

Paid daily scan digests on Telegram or email, plus data access for developers.

Education

Courses and webinars on reading OI, breadth and pivots.

Rough goal: 1,000 paying users × ₹500 = ₹5 lakh/month. Most sites need 12–24 months to get there.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Millions of new demat accounts in India
  • Repeat subscriptions, users come daily
  • End-of-day data keeps running costs low
  • Content and SEO bring free users

Cons

  • Crowded: Intraday Screener, Chartink, Screener.in already exist
  • Swing traders visit once a day, so you need evening alerts to bring them back
  • Users leave quickly if data is late or wrong
  • Strict SEBI rules; a bad month for markets means fewer users

How to win

  • Choose a narrow niche and do it better
  • Simple Hindi and English explanations
  • Very fast pages, clean mobile view
  • Honest, no-tips, education-first brand

Rules you must follow

  • Do not say "buy" or "sell" or give targets unless you are a SEBI-registered Research Analyst or Investment Adviser. Show data, not advice.
  • Do not copy exchange data or another site's data. Use a licensed vendor.
  • Add a risk disclaimer, privacy policy and terms on every page.
  • Follow SEBI rules on influencers and paid promotions. Disclose affiliate links.
  • Check the current rules with a lawyer before launch; they change often.
